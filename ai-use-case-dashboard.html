      Mission

      We turn Consumer Bank's AI capacity into a small number of capabilities that measurably change how customers bank with us and how the business runs.

      Clarity and prioritization are how we get there. One process for every AI idea, so capacity goes to what's worth building.

      One process· Clear accountability· Consistent measurement· Full transparency
      01

      Core principles

      Same questions, same scoring, every time — six themes that decide what we build and how we win.

      01

      Hypothesis first

      Start with what we believe and what would disprove it. Every use case states up front what would tell us to stop.

      02

      Evidence before scale

      Pilots exist to answer a question, not to prove we were right. Stop is a successful discovery — it cost four weeks, not two quarters.

      03

      Value and feasibility together

      Neither one alone gets a use case funded.

      04

      Finished, not launched

      Done means a business routine, a named owner, and measured value — not shipped.

      05

      Connected data

      Every stage, decision, and outcome is measured and visible, so we can manage the process and improve it.

      06

      Speed is a governance outcome

      Risk, Legal, and MRM engage early and by tier — controls are why we can move.

      02

      The lifecycle

      Eight stages — one accountability, one accountable role, one system of record each, with clear decision criteria for advancing. Intake, Triage, and Prioritization open a short playbook.

      Declining early is part of the process — declined and transferred outcomes stay in the portfolio as data.

      AI / Automation Use Case Intake

      Deliberately lightweight: decide whether a use case is worth building, not fully specify it. Detailed benefit, effort, and risk work happens after experimentation.

      Target: 15 minutes· Live answer coaching· Reviewed weekly· Jira is the system of record
      1 General information
      2 Business benefit
      3 Feasibility
      4 Risk flags

      Routing only — detailed risk review happens post-experimentation. Any flag routes to Risk or MRM before Approve; a prototype may still proceed in a controlled environment.

      Mock — in production this creates a Jira issue in the AI Use Case Capability.

      How this works

      • Fifteen minutes. Longer means we're asking for detail we haven't earned yet.
      • Ask only what the requester can answer — refinement comes after experimentation.
      • Declining early is part of the process. A bad idea is cheapest before anyone builds it.

      Where this goes

      • Every submission becomes a Jira issue and carries that ID forward.
      • Work is staffed and reported from Jira — if it's there, it's in the portfolio.
      • Declines get a Jira issue too — declines are portfolio data.

      What happens next

      • Reviewed at the weekly AI Intake Review, routed — never queued.
      • Leaves triage with a decision, an owner, and a date.
